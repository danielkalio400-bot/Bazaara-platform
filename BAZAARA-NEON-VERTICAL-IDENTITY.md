## Rules

1. Neon is an accent system, not a reason to sacrifice readability. Background, text and semantic status colors remain accessible.
2. Available vs Planned is communicated by text and status treatment, never by color alone.
3. Shopping, Grocery, Food and Business retain their existing application structures. The identity layer changes accents only.
4. Future applications inherit these tokens when their roadmap phase is implemented. Planned portal cards do not imply operational functionality.
5. Drive is intentionally monochrome: black surfaces, white accents and white glow. It should not borrow another vertical's hue.
6. Food and Pharmacy remain distinguishable: Food is red/coral-orange; Pharmacy is amber/orange.
7. Shared semantic colors (success, warning, error, information) are not overridden by vertical branding.
