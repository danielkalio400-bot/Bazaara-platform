# Bazaara Roadmaps 35, 38, 45, 47, 49 and 52

This package adds the requested Bazaara applications without changing the existing Shopping, Grocery or Food ports.

## Web ports
- Logistics: 3008
- Drive: 3009
- Pay: 3010
- Business: 3001 (expanded)
- Pharmacy: 3011
- Bazasport: 3012

## Native foundations
- apps/drive-rider-mobile
- apps/pay-mobile
- apps/bazasport-mobile

## Product boundaries
- Wallet shows development wallet/transfer simulations; real regulated financial movement remains disabled until provider/KYC/ledger policies are enabled.
- Pharmacy is OTC/wellness only. Prescription workflows are not activated by Roadmap 49.
- Bazasport implements Roadmap 52 sports content/scores. Real-money betting in Roadmap 53 is not activated.
- Logistics dispatch and Drive geospatial matching are represented as functional local product flows with production service hooks reserved for the later dispatch/pricing phases.
