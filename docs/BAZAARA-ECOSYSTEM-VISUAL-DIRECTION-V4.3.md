# BAZAARA ecosystem visual direction — V4.3

The supplied ecosystem boards are the canonical visual reference for future BAZAARA product work.

## Master brand

BAZAARA remains one master platform. BazID is the identity layer. Wallet is the money layer. Product names may carry their own functional accent, but typography, spacing, interaction quality, wordmark treatment, security posture and cross-product navigation must still feel like one BAZAARA system.

## Ecosystem 1 — Commerce & Everyday Life

Products represented in the current direction include Shopping, Food, Grocery, Drive, Logistics, Pay, Business, Pharmacy and Bazasport.

Primary visual language:
- clean white / ice surfaces for consumer and business contexts;
- deep BAZAARA navy as the structural colour;
- BAZAARA orange as the master commerce accent;
- charcoal / brushed-metal variants for operational and power-user surfaces;
- green reserved for successful/online/available state rather than as the master brand colour.

The current GO, Business, Operations and Pay work belongs to this ecosystem.

## Ecosystem 2 — Social, Media & Communication

BChat, BazClips, BazCircle, BazForum, BazTune, BazCut and BazSend.

Primary visual language:
- midnight / near-black immersive surfaces;
- violet, magenta and cyan neon accents;
- expressive creator-first imagery and motion;
- one BazID identity across all seven apps.

This visual identity should not leak into Ecosystem 1 operational products except where a shared master-platform element is intentionally used.

## Ecosystem 3 — Intelligence, Productivity & Infrastructure

Bazaara.com, Nova, Maps, Translate, News, Bmail, Meet, Calendar, Contacts, Docs, Sheets, Slides, Forms, Notes, Box, Vault, Photos, Studio, Bazaara AI, Spaces, Boards, Projects, Flow, Workspace, Sites, Code, Cloud, Creator Hub, Seller OS, Business OS and Bazaara One.

Primary visual language:
- white / ice productivity surfaces;
- deep navy structure;
- cobalt, teal and green functional accents;
- low-noise information density;
- strong desktop/tablet workspace behaviour;
- BazID and unified workspace patterns across the suite.

## Interaction rules

The visual system must remain premium and highly engaging without intentionally designing manipulative or compulsive mechanics. Retention should come from clarity, speed, usefulness, personalization, responsive feedback and polished motion.

All future work should preserve:
- cross-device adaptive layouts;
- large-screen density without mobile breakage;
- keyboard / pointer / touch accessibility;
- high-contrast states;
- visible loading, error, success and empty states;
- real data and backend enforcement rather than decorative controls;
- shared BazID, Pay, support, audit and role boundaries.
